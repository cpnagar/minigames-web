Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Games handles information when you use the Mini Games mobile application and this website.

1. About Mini Games

Mini Games is a casual entertainment application that provides classic board, puzzle, strategy, and number games. The app does not provide real-money gambling, betting, wagering, cash prizes, or monetary rewards.

2. Information We Collect

Mini Games is designed to work without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information.

Depending on the version of the app and the services enabled in that version, the application may process limited technical information such as device or app information needed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the service.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the core gameplay features.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as game settings, preferences, scores, progress, or other information required to provide the gameplay experience.

This information may remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the application.

4. Third-Party Services

Future or current versions of Mini Games may use third-party services for functions such as advertising, crash reporting, analytics, app distribution, or other technical services. Where such services are used, they may process information according to their own privacy policies.

If advertising or analytics services are added to a particular version of the app, the relevant data practices will be reflected in the app's Google Play Data Safety disclosures and applicable privacy information.

5. Advertising

If advertisements are displayed in a version of Mini Games, advertising providers may process certain information as necessary to provide, measure, personalize, or prevent abuse related to advertisements, subject to their applicable policies and your available privacy choices.

If the app version you use does not contain advertising, no advertising data is collected through the app's advertising functionality.

6. How Information Is Used

Information that may be processed is used only for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating the application;
  • Saving gameplay preferences and progress where applicable;
  • Maintaining application security and reliability;
  • Diagnosing crashes or technical problems where applicable;
  • Understanding application performance where applicable; and
  • Displaying advertisements where applicable.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, analyze, or provide features of the application, or where required by law.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Mini Games. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally on your device is generally controlled by you and can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

Where third-party services process technical or advertising information, retention is governed by the applicable third-party service policies and configurations.

10. Children's Privacy

Mini Games is a general-audience entertainment application. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Your Choices

You can stop using the application and uninstall it at any time. Depending on your device and the services available in the app, you may also be able to manage permissions, advertising preferences, or other privacy controls through your device settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, services, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
